## Research question and gap

Deep learning classifiers for viral nucleotide sequences perform well on benchmark accuracy but rarely reveal which genomic features drive a prediction. High accuracy can hide reliance on artefacts such as sequence length, nucleotide composition or database bias, so biological grounding remains unproven. A PRISMA 2020 systematic literature review (Scopus and PubMed, 16 October 2026; 1,621 records identified; 18 studies included) found that of 14 primary studies, only two quantified agreement between attributions and an external biological reference, seven made qualitative claims, five stated no evaluation, and none described a stability test or a deletion, insertion or retraining fidelity test.

The research objective is to evaluate the **stability**, **fidelity** and **biological plausibility** of feature attributions generated by deep learning-based viral genomic classification tools, and to deliver a reusable, model-agnostic evaluation pipeline.

### The download limit correction

The **actual download** does not use `--limit 500`. It uses `--limit 5000` and a **stratified sampler** before applying CAP. The reason is that `datasets summary` returns records in NCBI's internal order, not a random sample; taking the "first 87" would over-represent early submissions and, for Dengue, may over-represent one of the four serotypes. Taking the "first 500, then sample 87" would inherit the same bias at one remove.

The stratified sampler must:

1. Read `--limit 5000` from `datasets summary`.
2. Bucket records by **collection year** (from `isolate.collection_date`).
3. For Dengue, additionally bucket by **serotype** where extractable.
4. For SARS-CoV-2, additionally bucket by **lineage** where extractable.
5. Draw round-robin across buckets until CAP is reached.

This converts the dataset from "whatever NCBI returned first" to "a documented, stratified sample." That distinction is what a panel will notice.

## Locked technical decisions

Do not change these without explicit approval and a stated rationale.

- **Sequence retrieval:**
  - Probe: `datasets summary virus genome taxon <taxon> --complete-only --limit 500 --as-json-lines`
  - Download: `datasets summary virus genome taxon <taxon> --complete-only --limit 5000 --as-json-lines`, then `efetch -db nucleotide -id <accessions> -format fasta` in batches of 100.
- **Sampling:** stratified by collection year, seeded at 42, with additional stratification by serotype (Dengue) and lineage (SARS-CoV-2) where the metadata supports it. An `assert` must fail if any species returns fewer than CAP after fetching.
- **Balance:** every retained species contributes exactly CAP sequences.
- **Clustering:** CD-HIT-EST v4.8.1, `-c 0.90 -n 8 -M 0 -T 0`. Single threshold; do not re-cluster.
- **Split:** species-level (not cluster-level), with Ebola and Dengue as test, MERS and Yellow fever as validation, the five remaining species as training. Seeded at 42.
- **Leakage verification:** `cd-hit-2d` at 90% identity between test and train FASTA. Must return zero matches.
- **Models:** CNN (one-hot, 3,000 bp windows, stride 1,500), BiLSTM (6-mer tokenisation, same windowing), fine-tuned DNABERT-2 (HuggingFace `zhihan1996/DNABERT-2-117M`, byte-pair tokeniser, 512-token limit requiring per-family chunking: ~10 chunks for coronavirus genomes, ~4 for flavivirus genomes).
- **Attribution methods:** occlusion (50 bp window, 25 bp stride, neutral baseline), KernelSHAP (100-sequence background set, 500 coalition samples), windowed permutation (100 bp windows), Integrated Gradients (50% A/T/G/C baseline, 32 steps, on input for CNN/BiLSTM, embedding layer for DNABERT-2).
- **Consensus:** rank product across 3 models × 4 methods = 12 attributions per sequence, at 25 bp resolution, mapped to absolute genomic coordinates.
- **Stability:** 1,000 bootstrap resamples of test set; Spearman correlation; median and 95% percentile interval.
- **Fidelity:** deletion and insertion curves at 1%, 5%, 10%, 20% of sequence length; retraining without top 100 consensus positions vs. retraining without 100 random positions.
- **Controls:** shuffled labels, random-window baseline, randomised-model sanity check.
- **Plausibility:** hypergeometric enrichment of top 5% consensus positions in gene boundaries (from NCBI `cds.fna` or RefSeq feature tables), conserved domains (Pfam/InterProScan), and curated marker sites (SARS-CoV-2 lineage-defining substitutions, Dengue serotype-defining E substitutions, filovirus glycoprotein sites, analogous resources for Lassa and Nipah). Per-family scoring reported separately for held-out and in-distribution families.
- **Quality gate:** a model passes only if validation macro-F1 exceeds the majority-class baseline. Failing models are reported and flagged, not silently dropped.

## Non-negotiables

The following constraints govern every piece of work and cannot be relaxed without explicit approval:

1. **No data leakage.** Every split is verified with `cd-hit-2d` at the same 90% identity threshold used for clustering. A failed check halts progress; splits are regenerated, not patched.
2. **Balance.** Every retained species contributes exactly CAP sequences. The expansion script asserts this and exits on imbalance.
3. **Stratified sampling.** The download is not "first N of whatever NCBI returns." It is stratified by collection year, with additional stratification where the biology supports it (serotype for Dengue, lineage for SARS-CoV-2). This is the standard benchmark construction; a non-stratified sample is not.
4. **Reproducibility.** Every threshold (clustering identity, split seed, sampling seed, attribution window and stride, bootstrap count, enrichment foreground fraction) is pinned in a config file and recorded in `data/README.md`.
5. **Version pinning.** The ICTV VMR release is fixed at MSL41 v1 (2026-03-20). NCBI accessions are recorded, not just taxon names.
6. **Abstract-level honesty.** The systematic review's findings are abstract-level; any claim that a specific study did not report something is qualified as "did not report in its abstract," not "did not do."
7. **No unqualified priority claims.** Do not claim to be "the first" to apply any evaluation dimension. Deletion/insertion and stability tests exist in the broader XAI literature. The novelty claimed is the **combination** (stability + fidelity + plausibility, across multiple architectures and families, under a shared partition), not any single component.
8. **Scope discipline.** The classification task is five-way family, not species. The virus set is nine species across five families. Hendra is excluded and documented. Do not add species or expand scope without explicit instruction.
9. **Document consistency.** Every change to scope, split strategy, model choice or evaluation metric must be propagated to Chapters 1–4 and Appendix A. A proposal where Section 2.6 says "ten viruses" and Section 3.2 says "nine" will be marked down.
10. **Build standards.** No hardcoded paths outside the project root; no manual data manipulation outside the four pipeline scripts; no silent drop of failed accessions (all logged).
11. **Ethics and data.** Only public sequence data. No controlled-access or patient-identifiable data. No ethics approval required; this is stated explicitly in the repository README.
