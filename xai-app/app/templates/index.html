About Viral XAI

Viral XAI is a clinical-grade explainable AI (XAI) diagnostic dashboard designed for viral genomic surveillance. It goes beyond simple classification by evaluating how deep learning models make their decisions, ensuring they are biologically grounded and trustworthy.

The Three Pillars of XAI

  • Stability: Do different neural network architectures (CNN, BiLSTM, Transformer) focus on the same genomic regions? We measure this using Spearman correlation.
  • Fidelity: If we delete the regions the AI says are important, does the prediction break? We run live occlusion tests to find out.
  • Biological Plausibility: Does the AI focus on actual biological genes (e.g., the Spike protein, Polymerase), or is it looking at random genomic artifacts? We map attribution peaks to annotated GenBank genes.

Supported Viruses

Dengue, Chikungunya, Norovirus, Measles, Mpox, and Adenovirus C.
