About Viral XAI

Viral XAI is an interactive explanation dashboard designed for viral genomic surveillance. It goes beyond simple classification by evaluating how deep learning models make their decisions.

The Three Pillars of XAI

  • Stability: Spearman correlation between models' attribution rankings.
  • Fidelity: Drop in predicted probability when top windows are masked.
  • Plausibility: Fraction of top windows overlapping annotated genes.

Supported Viruses

Dengue, Chikungunya, Norovirus, Measles, Mpox, and Adenovirus C.
